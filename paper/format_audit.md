Source verification (2026-10-05): every arXiv identifier, repository URL, and commit in the manuscript was resolved; the numbers attributed to the calibration and scaling reports were read from the primary reports, not from the community index. The ACPs citation was verified against Crossref (DOI 10.1109/IC-NIDC67200.2025.11390349, 2025 9th International Conference on Network Intelligence and Digital Content), which gives the first author as Cong Li with a Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications affiliation. All 24 citations have a `\bibitem` entry generated from `draft.md`, with keys that name the entry they carry.

The figure PNGs are generated by `make_figures.py` and should be regenerated whenever the core CSV changes. The English and Chinese DOCX files were rebuilt, their three embedded images match `figures/` byte for byte, and they carry alt text.

LaTeX environment note: this host has TeX Live 2023/Debian but not the `texlive-fonts-extra` package, so `newtxtext`/`newtxmath` and their support macros (`xpatch`, `fontaxes`, `mweights`, `xstring`, `binhex`) were installed into the local tree `/data/caiyizhang/texmf`, with the font map registered in `/data/caiyizhang/texmf-var`. The host also has no `xelatex` binary, so the `xelatex` format was built from `xelatex.ini` into the same `TEXMFVAR`, and the Noto Serif/Sans CJK SC fonts were placed under `/data/caiyizhang/texmf/fonts/opentype/public/noto` with a fontconfig file. Both PDFs were produced with those variables set; the exact commands are in `submission_checklist.md`. A stock TeX Live full installation needs no such workaround.

Three build-script defects were found and fixed by this audit: generated `equation` environments contained blank lines that are fatal in math mode; long `\\texttt` identifiers had no break opportunities (now cut into short runs); and the LNCS bibliography was a hard-coded nine-entry list, now generated from the Markdown reference list. A fourth analysis defect was corrected: the ECE implementation had counted every probability below 0.9 again in the final bin, and the threshold routine had mislabeled a 0.99 fallback as a certification when no development threshold met the target. Both PDFs now build with 0 overfull boxes, and the per-table column widths live in `TABLE_COLUMNS` in `build_lncs.py`, keyed by the table number in document order.
