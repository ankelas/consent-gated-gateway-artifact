## Evidence in the current manuscript

The paper reports one live deployment, not a simulator.

- **Routing.** 130 model-generated requests (50 direct, 40 ambiguous, 40 out-of-scope) delivered through the real iMessage/Photon ingress and the ACPs adapter, three times each. Direct hit rate 48/48/50 of 50; ambiguous requests recommended more than one agent in 39/38/38 of 40; out-of-scope requests never recommended a study partner (0/40) and always recommended another public-registry agent (40/40).
- **Consent and effect.** 30 consent trials plus 10 redelivery trials on the deployed adapter, with the adapter's host-name check aligned to the ACPs URI-SAN identity. A study partner was the first candidate in 36 trials; none reached a partner before consent (0/40); each of the 36 consents produced exactly one invocation and exactly one partner action; 20 replayed consent events produced no action and returned the stored reply. Partner actions are recorded by `EFFECT_OUTBOX=/outbox/effects.jsonl` in `acps-public-demo/roster/compose.yml`.
- **Invariant ablations.** Identity (0/30 control vs 30/30 ablated), deduplication (30 vs 60 executions), and isolation (0/30 vs 30/30 cross-caller reads), each paired with a same-code control built from the same staging copy. Staging sources live in `/data/caiyizhang/ablation/`.
- **Judgment layer.** A 280-item generated suite with a 62-item held-out split, measured on one deployed model (`deepseek-flash`) across three answer shapes, plus a 456-item injection stress set (67 injected / 47 benign after adjudication). The endpoint is not deterministic, so each suite is sampled three times and reported as a mean with its observed range. The reported operating point is 0.99.
- ACPs means **Agent Collaboration Protocols**, with AIP as **Agent Interaction Protocol**, AIC as **Agent Identity Code**, and ACS as **Agent Capability Specification**, pinned to `ACPs-spec-v02.02`, SDK `v2.2.0`, commit `896e4bc79b95f11589e92eab06322430e5c64505`. Section 2.2 cites the framework to Li et al., IC-NIDC 2025 (DOI `10.1109/IC-NIDC67200.2025.11390349`), and the implementation to the repository [13].

The evidence boundary is stated in §9.5: one deployment, one registry, one capability set, and a generator-labelled judgment suite; wide-area transport and a second deployment are not measured.
